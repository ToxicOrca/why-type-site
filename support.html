Why Type

Reusable email templates for Microsoft Outlook — stop typing the same thing twice.

What it does

Why Type lets you create, organize, and search reusable text or HTML templates ("Why Types") and insert them into an email you're composing with a single click. You can group templates into categories, expand a typed keyword into a full template, and import or export your whole library as a JSON file.

Quick start

Open a new email, then click Open Why Type on the Message ribbon. Click any template in the panel to insert it at your cursor. Click Edit to open the manager, where you can create templates, organize categories, and set a keyword for each template. To use a keyword: type it in the email body (for example hello), select it, and click Expand Why Type in the ribbon — the keyword is replaced with the full template.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my templates stored? Locally, in your Outlook add-in storage on your own device or mailbox profile. They are never uploaded to us — see the privacy policy.

Can I back up or move my templates? Yes — use the Export button at the bottom of the panel to save a JSON file, and Import to load one (duplicates are skipped automatically).

Does it work in plain-text and HTML emails? Templates can be plain text (formatting and line breaks preserved) or raw HTML — toggle "Use HTML formatting" per template in the manager.

Why don't I see the buttons? The add-in appears only while composing a message (new email, reply, or forward), not while reading one.
