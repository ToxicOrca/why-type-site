Why Type – Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Why Type is an Outlook add-in for creating and inserting reusable email templates. This policy describes what information the add-in handles. The short version: your data stays with you — Why Type has no servers, no accounts, and collects nothing.

What data Why Type handles

The templates you create (their names, categories, keywords, and body text) are stored locally using Microsoft Office's add-in storage on your device or Outlook profile. This data never leaves your Outlook environment: the add-in does not transmit your templates, your emails, or any personal information to the developer or to any third party.

What we collect

Nothing. Why Type contains no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no advertising, and no account system. The developer has no ability to see your templates or your email content.

Email access

Why Type requests the Office permission to read and write the message you are composing. This is used solely to insert the template you choose at your cursor position and, when you use keyword expansion, to read the text you have selected. The add-in does not read, store, or transmit any other part of your mailbox.

Hosting

The add-in's pages are static files served from GitHub Pages (a GitHub, Inc. service). Like any web host, GitHub may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) when the pages load; see GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not receive or use this information.

Third-party links

The add-in contains an optional link to the developer's Ko-fi page for voluntary tips. Ko-fi is an independent service with its own privacy policy; no information is shared with Ko-fi unless you choose to visit it.

Your data, your control

You can export your templates at any time from the add-in's Export button, and delete them by removing templates in the manager or uninstalling the add-in. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If the add-in's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before the change takes effect.
